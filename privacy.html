Advertising provider data When advertising is enabled, UMP information is refreshed; the advertising SDK is not initialised until requests are permitted. The switch stops new ad requests; provider operations in progress may finish and historical provider data is not automatically deleted. The privacy button opens Google’s preference form when required. 9. Sponsor measurement and marketing Sponsor view/click measurement is disabled by default. This is not verified audience measurement or comprehensive fraud detection. Dismissal or leaving the screen requires a new choice. Do not send unnecessary documents or sensitive information beyond the subject of your request and the relevant account.
